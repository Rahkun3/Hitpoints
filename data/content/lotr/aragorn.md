In Tolkien's books, Aragorn is the son of Arathorn II and Gilraen. Raised in Rivendell as Estel, he learns his name and lineage from Elrond. He is 87 at the start of the War of the Ring: Chieftain of the Dúnedain, a Ranger of the North with Númenórean longevity, and bearer of the shards of Narsil, later reforged as Andúril. He meets the hobbits as Strider in Bree and joins the Fellowship. He is betrothed to Arwen Undómiel; that romance sits largely in Appendix A, not the main chapters. Book Aragorn is comparatively assured of kingship and does not refuse it; after the War he is crowned Elessar of the Reunited Kingdom.

In Jackson's films, Viggo Mortensen plays him as more reluctant, and Arwen is written into the main plot. The reforging of Narsil is delayed. Elves at Helm's Deep are a Film addition; the warg-cliff sequence and the path of the Army of the Dead also diverge. He is not a Hobbit-trilogy protagonist.

Amazon's series is Second Age and is not his story. Aragorn himself does not appear.
