In Tolkien's books, Rivendell (Imladris) was founded by Elrond in the Second Age — commonly dated SA 1697 — after the fall of Eregion in the war with Sauron. It is hidden in a valley of the Bruinen (Loudwater) in the western foothills of the Misty Mountains. It is a place of lore, song, healing, and memory, not a fortress-city. Bilbo visits in *The Hobbit*. In *The Lord of the Rings*, Frodo is brought there wounded; the Council of Elrond chooses the Ring's path; the Fellowship departs. Aragorn was raised here as Estel. Elrond's children are Arwen, Elladan, and Elrohir.

In Jackson's films, the Last Homely House is layered architecture in a gorge (Kaitoke Regional Park and studio). The Council is a set-piece. Arwen's ford ride is Film: in the book Frodo rides Glorfindel's horse Asfaloth, and the flood of the Bruinen is tied to Elrond, with Gandalf's contribution in the text.

Amazon's series is Second Age. Jackson's Rivendell is not a Series set. Season 2 ends in an unnamed sanctuary valley after Eregion falls; it is not named Imladris on screen. Season 3 is unaired — do not treat that valley as the founding of Rivendell. *The Lord of the Rings Online* and other games use it as a hub (Game track).
