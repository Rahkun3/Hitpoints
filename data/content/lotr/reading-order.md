**First-time literary order** (Tolkien’s published fiction — not films or series):

1. *The Hobbit* (1937) — tone, Bilbo, and the Ring’s first appearance.
2. *The Lord of the Rings* (1954–55) — one novel in three volumes: *The Fellowship of the Ring*, *The Two Towers*, *The Return of the King*. After *The Return of the King*, read the Appendices, especially A (kings and rulers) and B (the Tale of Years).
3. *The Silmarillion* (1977) — optional next; mythic history, not a novel. See that guide.
4. *Unfinished Tales* (1980).
5. Later narrative publications if wanted: *The Children of Húrin*, *Beren and Lúthien*, *The Fall of Gondolin*, *The Fall of Númenor*. These overlap *Silmarillion* material and are not required.

Some readers start with *The Lord of the Rings* and treat *The Hobbit* as an optional prequel. This guide still recommends *The Hobbit* first for tone and for Bilbo. Do not send first-timers into *The History of Middle-earth*: those volumes are draft scholarship, not a reading path.

Jackson film order is theatrical release order (LotR then Hobbit) or story order (Hobbit then LotR) — see Jackson Films. Amazon’s *The Rings of Power* is not a reading path.
