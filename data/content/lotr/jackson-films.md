Peter Jackson directed six live-action films for New Line. *The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring* (2001), *The Two Towers* (2002), and *The Return of the King* (2003) adapt Tolkien’s novel. Principal cast includes Elijah Wood (Frodo), Viggo Mortensen (Aragorn), Ian McKellen (Gandalf), Cate Blanchett (Galadriel), and Sean Astin (Sam). *The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey* (2012), *The Desolation of Smaug* (2013), and *The Battle of the Five Armies* (2014) adapt *The Hobbit*, drawing also on the LotR Appendices.

Theatrical cuts and **Extended Editions** both sit on the **Film** track. Extended Editions add book-adjacent scenes; they are still not the books.

In Jackson’s films, labeled divergences include: no Tom Bombadil; the seventeen-year gap after Bilbo’s party is condensed; Arwen’s role is expanded; Faramir’s temptation is staged at Osgiliath; Elves appear at Helm’s Deep; the Scouring of the Shire is omitted (theatrical and Extended). The Hobbit films add Tauriel, extra Dol Guldur plot, and Azog as a recurring hunter.

This page is not Amazon’s *The Rings of Power* (a **Series** track) and not games. Ralph Bakshi’s 1978 animated *The Lord of the Rings* is a separate earlier adaptation.
