In Tolkien's books, the Shire is Hobbit-country in Eriador, settled in TA 1601 by Hobbits who crossed from Bree-land. It was originally a grant from the King at Fornost in Arnor. The land is organized in four farthings — North, South, East, and West — with Michel Delving as chief town. Hobbiton, Bywater, and Tuckborough lie in the west; Buckland sits across the Brandywine on the East-farthing border and is often grouped with Shire culture. Bounders and Shirriffs keep the borders. Hobbits take little interest in outside politics until the War of the Ring. The book includes the Scouring of the Shire, with Saruman as Sharkey.

In Jackson's films, Hobbiton was built in Matamata, New Zealand, and supplies the idyllic opening of both trilogies. The Scouring is omitted from both theatrical and Extended Editions — the Extended Edition kills Saruman at Orthanc instead — so *The Lord of the Rings* returns to an un-scoured Shire.

Amazon's *The Rings of Power* is Second Age and does not use the Shire: Hobbits have not yet settled it. The series' wandering Harfoots are Second Age folk in Rhovanion, not the Third Age Shire. Starter zones in *The Lord of the Rings Online* and similar titles are Game track.
