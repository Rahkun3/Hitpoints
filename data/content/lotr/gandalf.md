In Tolkien's books, Gandalf is a Maia; in Valinor he is called Olórin. He is one of five Istari sent to Middle-earth — not a human old man except in guise. He bears a staff and Narya, the Ring of Fire, given by Círdan. He prompts Bilbo's adventure; later he identifies the One Ring and sends Frodo out. He falls in Moria opposing the Balrog and returns as Gandalf the White.

In Jackson's films, Ian McKellen plays him in both trilogies. The visual language of the Eye and the Balrog is Film. The Hobbit films expand the White Council and Dol Guldur plot beyond the book's there-and-back-again frame.

In Amazon's The Rings of Power, Season 2 names the Stranger **Gandalf** (Series, not Book history). Most Book accounts place the Istari's arrival in the Third Age; late notes that differ on wizard arrivals concern the **Blue Wizards**, not Olórin as a Second-Age Istar. Licensed games often use him as an advisor NPC (Game track).
