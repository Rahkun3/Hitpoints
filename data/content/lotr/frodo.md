In Tolkien's books, Frodo is the cousin and adopted heir of Bilbo Baggins (Bilbo calls him nephew). He comes of age at 33 at the Farewell Party in TA 3001, but does not leave the Shire until he is 50, in TA 3018. He inherits Bag End and the Ring; Gandalf confirms it is the One. He leaves with Samwise Gamgee; Merry and Pippin join. After the Council of Elrond he is named Ring-bearer of the Fellowship. When the company breaks, the quest continues with Sam. It ends at Orodruin, where Gollum is bound up with the Ring. Afterward Frodo cannot settle; he sails West from the Grey Havens with the Ring-bearers.

In Jackson's films, Elijah Wood plays him as clearly younger in look, and the 17-year gap is compressed. The films omit Tom Bombadil, take a more direct flight toward Bree and Rivendell, and omit the Scouring in both the theatrical cut and the Extended Edition, so the Shire he returns to is un-scoured on screen. The Bag End framing with old Bilbo in *An Unexpected Journey* is Film, not a scene from Tolkien's novel.

Amazon's series is Second Age; Frodo is not a Series lead. Licensed games sometimes make him playable or central; those remain Game track.
