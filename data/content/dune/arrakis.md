## Overview

Water is scarce; life adapts through stillsuits, sietches, and careful ecology. The Fremen are the planet's people of the open desert; offworld Houses fight for spice harvesting rights under the Padishah Emperor and the Guild's transport monopoly. Sandworms ([[shai-hulud|Shai-Hulud]]) dominate the deep desert and are inseparable from the spice cycle.

- **Resource:** Melange — see [[spice|Spice]].
- **Ecology:** Extreme aridity; worms; terraforming dreams among Fremen and others.
- **Politics:** Great House fiefs, CHOAM, Guild, and Imperial interest.

## Continuity notes

> **Shared setting:** Arrakis as desert spice-world is common across Book and Film. Ecological and political detail is densest in Herbert; Villeneuve emphasizes scale and ritual visually.
