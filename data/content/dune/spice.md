## Overview

Found only on [[arrakis|Arrakis]] in known space, spice is the prize of Great House politics, CHOAM economics, and Spacing Guild monopoly. Geriatric and cognitive effects make withdrawal catastrophic at Imperial scale. Visual cue across media: the blue-within-blue eyes of heavy users.

- **Life:** Extends lifespan; addictive.
- **Travel:** Enables Guild Navigators' spacefolding.
- **Sight:** Blue-within-blue irises with sustained use.

## Continuity notes

> **Book-primary:** Mechanisms and politics of spice are densest in Herbert. Films and series keep the high-level effects (life, Guild, eyes) as Shared shorthand.
