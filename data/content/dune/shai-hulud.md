## Overview

Worms respond to rhythmic vibration on the open sand; Fremen culture builds around respect, risk, and ritual in worm country. At a high level (spoiler-light): spice production is tied to the worm life cycle and Arrakis's desert chemistry — details deepen across Herbert's novels and are only sketched in most adaptations.

- **Ecology:** Desert megafauna; territorial open sand.
- **Culture:** Fremen reverence and naming (Shai-Hulud).
- **Spice:** Linked to the worm cycle — see [[spice|Melange]].

## Continuity notes

> **Book-primary ecology:** Full spice–worm cycle is literary canon. Films depict worms as spectacle and threat; keep deep cycle spoilers on Book pages.
