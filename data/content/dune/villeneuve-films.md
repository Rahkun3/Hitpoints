Denis Villeneuve's two-part cycle is a theatrical adaptation of the first novel, *Dune* (1965) — not of Herbert's six-book sequence, and not interchangeable with Lynch 1984.

**Dune: Part One (2021):** runs Caladan to Arrakis: Yueh's betrayal, Duke Leto's death, desert exile, the Jamis combat, and a close at Sietch Tabr ("this is only the beginning"). It omits the banquet, Fenring, full Guild politics, and the book's inner-monologue style. Cast anchors: Timothée Chalamet (Paul), Rebecca Ferguson (Jessica), Zendaya (Chani), Oscar Isaac (Leto), Javier Bardem (Stilgar), Stellan Skarsgård (the Baron).

**Dune: Part Two (2024):** covers Fremen war, the Water of Life, a North/South split (Film-only), Feyd-Rautha's arena (Austin Butler), the Baron, and the assault on Arrakeen. Chani's break at the end is Film-only; the book keeps a political marriage to Irulan (Florence Pugh) while Chani remains. Alia speaks in utero here; Book has child Alia kill the Baron — do not swap those beats. Dave Bautista plays Rabban.

*Dune: Part Three* adapts *Dune Messiah*. Principal photography wrapped 2025; US/Canada release is dated **18 December 2026**. As of 28 August 2026 it is **not released** — not Book, not live Film continuity yet. Do not treat trailers or production reporting as wiki canon.
