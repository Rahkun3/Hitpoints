The **Spacing Guild** holds the Imperium's practical leash: a monopoly on foldspace travel. Guild Navigators, mutated by spice (**mélange**), plot Heighliner courses. CHOAM, the Emperor, and the Great Houses cannot move armies or cargo without Guild terms. They are not "just pilots." Shared: spice-dependent Navigators and Imperial dependence on Guild transport.

**Book:** mostly offstage pressure — Heighliners, the Guild Bank, spice-dependence as a veto on war. Navigators are rumored and rarely seen; their mutation is a Book-primary mechanism, not a crowd-facing office. Holtzman engines fold space; Navigators need mélange-prescience to plot a safe path. Without spice the monopoly collapses. Arrakis is the Guild's choke point as much as the Emperor's.

**Film (Lynch):** a giant third-stage Navigator in a tank is highly visible, plus Guildsmen at court — Lynch staging, not Book default. **Film (Villeneuve):** Heighliners are shown at scale; Navigators are largely absent on-screen in *Part One* and *Part Two* — a Film-Villeneuve omission, not a Book fact.

Do not treat either film's visibility choice as Herbert's political design.
