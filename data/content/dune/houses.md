**Great Houses** are the aristocratic spine of the Imperium: siridar fiefs, CHOAM shares, and voting weight in the **Landsraad**, under the **faufreluches** class order. For v1 three Houses matter.

**House Atreides:** honor, Caladan, a military elite (Idaho, Halleck, Hawat). Duke Leto; Lady Jessica as bound concubine, not wife; Paul as heir.

**House Harkonnen:** Giedi Prime, spice profit, brutality. Baron Vladimir; Rabban; Feyd-Rautha.

**House Corrino:** Padishah Emperor Shaddam IV; court world Kaitain; **Salusa Secundus** as prison and Sardaukar proving ground. Shaddam lacks a male heir; the Bene Gesserit hold his daughters, including Irulan.

**Book:** this structure is Herbert's political machine — CHOAM, Landsraad, and Imperial Sardaukar as the three-way leash. **Film:** both Lynch and Villeneuve keep the three-house war; they compress Landsraad and CHOAM. **BH/KJA:** the *House* prequel trilogy (*House Atreides*, *House Harkonnen*, *House Corrino*) is an optional separate track — not Book.

Shared names and roles; do not mash prequel plots into the 1965 novel.
