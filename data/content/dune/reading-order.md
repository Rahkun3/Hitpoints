## Frank Herbert core (recommended)

1. *Dune* (1965)
2. *Dune Messiah* (1969)
3. *Children of Dune* (1976)
4. *God Emperor of Dune* (1981)
5. *Heretics of Dune* (1984)
6. *Chapterhouse: Dune* (1985)

Publication order is the cleanest first path. Later books assume earlier ones; do not start mid-cycle for a first read.

## Optional separate track

Novels by **Brian Herbert and Kevin J. Anderson** sit outside this wiki's v1 primary literary canon. Treat as optional after the Frank Herbert six.

## Films & series

Theatrical films (Lynch 1984; Villeneuve Part One / Two) and TV adaptations illustrate or compress the books — they do not replace reading *Dune* and its sequels. See [[films|Films]].
