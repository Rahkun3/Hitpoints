## Overview

His path mixes prophecy, House politics, and desert war: breeding-program stakes, Atreides–Harkonnen rivalry, and Fremen alliance. Shared core: Atreides heir, Jessica's son, Muad'Dib. How prophecy and consequence unfold is Book-primary.

## Continuity notes

**Book:** denser prophetic arc across *Dune* and sequels. **Film:** Villeneuve compresses the first novel across Part One / Two; Lynch 1984 is a separate take. Label Film when pacing diverges.

> **Book-primary:** Prefer Herbert for identity and long-arc outcomes. Villeneuve films are adaptations with their own pacing — not substitutes for the novels.
