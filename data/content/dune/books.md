## Overview

The cycle opens with *Dune* (1965) and closes Herbert's run with *Chapterhouse: Dune* (1985). Later novels by Brian Herbert and Kevin J. Anderson expand the setting; for v1 they are noted separately, not merged into primary canon badges.

## Frank Herbert — six novels

1. *Dune* (1965)
2. *Dune Messiah* (1969)
3. *Children of Dune* (1976)
4. *God Emperor of Dune* (1981)
5. *Heretics of Dune* (1984)
6. *Chapterhouse: Dune* (1985)
