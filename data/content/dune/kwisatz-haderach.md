**Kwisatz Haderach** means "shortening of the way": the Bene Gesserit's intended male who can look where Reverend Mothers cannot — both male and female ancestral lines, Other Memory in full. He is the breeding-program end product, not a desert savior title and not "the Chosen One."

Jessica producing a son for Duke Leto brings a candidate a generation early. Paul is a possible Kwisatz Haderach; the novels complicate whether he is the one the Sisterhood wanted. Do not collapse the term into **Lisan al-Gaib**: that is a Fremen prophecy planted by the Missionaria Protectiva. The two instruments meet in Paul; they are not the same.

**SPOILER (light):** Alia, Jessica's daughter, is adjacent as an **abomination** in Sisterhood terms — pre-born to Other Memory.

**Book:** the genetic program, Other Memory, and the question of whether Paul is the KH they designed remain literary canon. **Film (Villeneuve):** Mohiam and Jessica use the term; the program's metaphysics are not fully staged. **Film (Lynch):** the term is used; treat it as an adaptation label, not a full Book definition.

Later novels keep asking whether Paul is the Kwisatz Haderach the Sisterhood wanted — a sequel pointer, not a Film fact.
