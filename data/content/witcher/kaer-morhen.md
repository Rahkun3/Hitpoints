## Overview

**Kaer Morhen** is the Wolf School fortress. Boys were mutated here through the **Trial of the Grasses**; most died. Survivors gained strength, senses, slowed aging, and Signs. A pogrom decades before the saga (wiki / *Crossroads of Ravens* dating ~1194; clashes with Triss's recollection in *Blood of Elves*) killed most witchers and students; mutation secrets were largely lost. Vesemir was among the survivors.

By the saga the keep is a ruin staffed by a handful: Vesemir, Geralt, Eskel, Lambert, and visiting witcher Coën (school unnamed in Sapkowski; Griffin is fan anthology *Szpony i kły* / games). In *Blood of Elves* they hide and train [[ciri|Ciri]]. Books firmly establish the Wolf keep; detailed Order-to-schools genealogy is largely game / companion expansion.

## Continuity notes

**Book:** Vesemir survives the saga. **Games:** home base across the trilogy — assault and mutagen theft open *The Witcher* (2007); *Wild Hunt* returns for Ciri's rescue. Vesemir dies at the keep in *Wild Hunt* (TW3). **Netflix:** Eskel dies at the keep (S2). Vesemir dies at Montecalvo (S4), not the keep.

> **Shared core:** Book keep of the Wolf school; major game hub across TW1–3. Netflix staging and deaths are parallel, not merges.
