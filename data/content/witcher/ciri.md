## Overview

Across Book, Game, and Netflix she is the hinge of the saga: hunted for her bloodline, capable of walking between worlds, and bound to Geralt and Yennefer as found family. Exact paths diverge by continuity — treat game endings and Netflix arcs as separate tracks.

## Continuity notes

> **Shared core:** Law of Surprise, Elder Blood, and the Geralt–Yennefer bond. Sword training at Kaer Morhen is book; she does not undergo the Trial of the Grasses. **Book:** Aen Elle imprisonment (*The Tower of the Swallow* / *The Lady of the Lake*) is captivity, not schooling. **Game:** Avallac’h as companion/mentor, elf-as-education, and Ciri-as-witcher endings are Game. Netflix's timeline is not interchangeable with the novels.
