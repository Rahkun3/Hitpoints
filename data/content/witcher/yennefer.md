## Overview

Born Beltane 1173 in Vengerberg (Aedirn) with a congenital hunchback; trained at **Aretuza** under Tissaia de Vries. Tradition magically corrected her body; slight asymmetry remains. Violet eyes, black hair, black-and-white dress, lilac-and-gooseberry scent. Blinded at Sodden Hill, sight later restored. Book sorcerers are sterile as a class — she hunts cures. (**Netflix** frames sterility as a chosen sacrifice; not book-canon.)

She meets Geralt in "The Last Wish"; his third wish (never quoted) saves her and binds their fates. Possessive, ambitious, sarcastic. At Ellander she becomes Ciri's magic teacher and a mother in all but blood. Lodge membership is late and uneasy — not a resident Aedirn-court identity.

## Continuity notes

**Games:** largely absent from TW1–2; primary love interest in *Wild Hunt*. **Netflix:** Anya Chalotra; Aretuza youth largely show-invented. Saga endings omitted here.

> **Shared core:** Djinn binding (third wish never quoted), sorceress identity, Ciri bond. Book sterility (as a class; she hunts cures) vs Netflix chosen sacrifice. Romance and political arcs differ across books, games (player choice), and Netflix.
