## Overview

Showrunner **Lauren Schmidt Hissrich** adapted the novels, not the games. Premiere 20 December 2019.

## Main series

- **S1** (2019) — *Last Wish* / *Sword of Destiny*, nonlinear; **Henry Cavill**.
- **S2** (2021) — toward *Blood of Elves*.
- **S3** (2023) — *Time of Contempt*; Cavill's last.
- **S4** (2025) — *Baptism* / *Tower of the Swallow*; **Liam Hemsworth**.
- **S5** (final) — remaining saga.

Cast: Anya Chalotra (Yennefer), Freya Allan (Ciri), Joey Batey (Jaskier). Spin-offs include *Nightmare of the Wolf*, *Blood Origin*, *Sirens of the Deep*. Divergences include three-lead S1 and changed fates (e.g. Eskel). Polish *Hexer* (2001–02) is separate.
