## Overview

CDPR treats the games as **non-canonical sequels** after *The Lady of the Lake* (Wild Hunt / amnesia bridge). Sapkowski: a free adaptation by other authors, not a sequel to his novels.

## Mainline

- *The Witcher* (2007) — amnesiac Geralt, Vizima.
- *Assassins of Kings* (2011) — Letho; Roche/Iorveth fork.
- *Wild Hunt* (2015) — Ciri and the Hunt; expansions **Hearts of Stone** (2015), **Blood and Wine** (2016).

**Upcoming:** *The Witcher IV* — Ciri lead, new trilogy. Also *Gwent*, *Thronebreaker*, other spin-offs. Drop game-only facts when returning to the books.

## Continuity notes

> **Game continuity:** CDPR continuity after the saga; not Sapkowski literary canon. Amnesia and Wild Hunt events belong here, not in Book articles.
