Recommended first-time path.

**Play (RTS spine):**

1. *Warcraft: Orcs & Humans* (First War)
2. *Warcraft II: Tides of Darkness* (Second War)
3. *Warcraft II: Beyond the Dark Portal*
4. *Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos* (Third War)
5. *Warcraft III: The Frozen Throne*

**Then WoW:** treat as a setting continuation after Frozen Throne. Play or read eras in release order: Classic, The Burning Crusade, Wrath of the Lich King, Cataclysm, Mists of Pandaria, Warlords of Draenor, Legion, Battle for Azeroth, Shadowlands, Dragonflight, The War Within, Midnight. This is not a leveling guide.

**Novels** (optional glue, not required): *The Last Guardian*; *Lord of the Clans*; *Day of the Dragon*; *Rise of the Horde*; *Of Blood and Honor*; *Tides of Darkness* / *Beyond the Dark Portal* novelizations; *Arthas: Rise of the Lich King*; *Cycle of Hatred*; *Tides of War*. They fill gaps; they are not a separate plot like a rival canon.

**Film:** 2016 *Warcraft* -- optional, WC1-inspired, not on the main play path.
