**World of Warcraft** (2004-) is Blizzard's MMORPG setting. It continues years after *Warcraft III: The Frozen Throne*. On **Azeroth**, a Stormwind-led Alliance faces an Orgrimmar-led Horde. The Burning Legion, the Scourge, and later threats arrive as expansion eras, not as a patch list or class guide.

Major eras by name: Classic, The Burning Crusade, Wrath of the Lich King, Cataclysm, Mists of Pandaria, Warlords of Draenor, Legion, Battle for Azeroth, Shadowlands, Dragonflight, The War Within, **Midnight** (live March 2026). This page is a setting index.

**WoW** sometimes retcons **RTS** campaign geography and cosmology (broader Titan / worldsoul framing; Chronicle is a WoW-era synthesis). Official **Novel** books often novelize or bridge eras. Shared names with Warcraft III do not make Icecrown Citadel, Theramore's destruction, or Argus unmarked RTS facts. For play order, see **Reading and Play Order**.
