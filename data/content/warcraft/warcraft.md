**Warcraft** is Blizzard Entertainment's fantasy franchise: RTS campaigns (1994-2003), then **World of Warcraft** (2004-), plus official novels and a 2016 film. Shared names -- **Azeroth**, the **Horde**, the **Alliance**, **Arthas** -- do not mean one unmarked timeline.

Three tracks stay labeled. **RTS** is *Warcraft: Orcs & Humans*, *Warcraft II: Tides of Darkness*, *Warcraft II: Beyond the Dark Portal*, *Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos*, and *Warcraft III: The Frozen Throne*. Those campaigns cover the First, Second, and Third Wars. **WoW** continues years after Frozen Throne as a living MMO setting; it sometimes retcons RTS geography and cosmology. **Novel** books (Christie Golden and others) fill gaps between games. They are optional glue, not a required rival canon. **Film** means only the 2016 Duncan Jones movie -- a WC1-inspired adaptation, not RTS or WoW game canon.

For a first path, play the RTS spine, then treat WoW as a setting continuation by expansion era. See **Reading and Play Order**. Do not collapse the WC1 southern human kingdom named Azeroth with the later planet of Azeroth. Do not present later MMO raid outcomes, Shadowlands, or later Horde politics as unmarked RTS facts.
