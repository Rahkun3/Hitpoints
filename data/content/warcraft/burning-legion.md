**The Burning Legion** is a demonic army serving **Sargeras**. **RTS** *Warcraft III* is where the name is campaign-central; it names commanders **Archimonde** and **Kil'jaeden** (eredar). In the Third War the plague and Scourge serve as a path for the Legion; Archimonde is summoned at Dalaran and defeated at Mount Hyjal.

**RTS** WC1/2: orcs invade Azeroth through the Dark Portal. Later telling (**Novel** / Chronicle / **WoW**) attributes their fel corruption to Kil'jaeden and **Gul'dan**. That is not original WC1 box copy; label it as later lore.

**WoW:** Burning Crusade (Outland; Kil'jaeden); Legion expansion (Broken Isles, Argus). Era names only -- not a raid guide. Argus / Antorus are **WoW**, not WC3 campaign text. Keep Titan cosmology light; Sargeras as ordered-titan backstory is **WoW** / Chronicle synthesis if used at all.
