**Warcraft III** is the RTS spine of the Third War. *Reign of Chaos* (2002) and *The Frozen Throne* (2003) are the story on this page.

*Reign of Chaos:* the Plague of Lordaeron; **Arthas Menethil**'s fall; the Scourge; the Horde's exodus to Kalimdor under **Thrall**; Alliance remnants under **Jaina Proudmoore**; the night elves; the Battle of Mount Hyjal, where a three-way alliance defeats **Archimonde**. The Prophet (**Medivh**) warns Thrall and Jaina to sail west. The Scourge is the Legion's path into Lordaeron.

*The Frozen Throne:* **Illidan**, the naga, and **Maiev**; **Kael'thas** and the blood elves; **Sylvanas** frees herself and takes the Lordaeron ruins. Arthas reaches Icecrown, defeats Illidan, and becomes the Lich King (merging with **Ner'zhul**). The Rexxar campaign shows the founding of **Durotar**.

**WoW**'s setting continues years later -- see **World of Warcraft**. Do not read Wrath of the Lich King, the Icecrown Citadel raid, or Bolvar as Lich King into this game.
