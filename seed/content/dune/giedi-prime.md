**Giedi Prime** is the Harkonnen homeworld: industrial, oppressive, and the seat of Baron Vladimir Harkonnen's power. Where Caladan is water and honor, Giedi Prime is extraction, arena spectacle, and a slave-culture that feeds the House's spice profit on Arrakis. Shared: Harkonnen planet, brutal industry, contrast with Atreides Caladan.

**Book:** oiliness of atmosphere and manners; a gladiatorial arena; Feyd-Rautha as the Baron's favored nephew, trained as a public blade. The world's culture is the House's culture — appetite, fear, and display.

**Film (Villeneuve):** Feyd's birthday combat is shot in a black-and-white infrared arena aesthetic — Film-Villeneuve visual language, not a Book description of the planet's light. **Film (Lynch):** an industrial hell with a different design grammar, including Lynch-only body horror on the Baron himself (boils, heart plugs) that should not be copied onto Book Giedi Prime.

Planetary ecology beyond that oppression is not required for v1. The Baron's war is fought for Arrakis; Giedi Prime is the factory floor behind it.
