**Caladan** is the ancestral seat of House Atreides: an ocean world of rain, **pundi rice**, and Castle Caladan. It is the climate opposite of Arrakis — water in excess, a ducal culture of loyalty, and a military elite trained in wet-world conditions. Paul and Jessica leave it when the Padishah Emperor assigns the Arrakis fief. Shared: Atreides homeworld, ocean, departure.

**Book:** culture and economy stay Book-primary — pundi rice, fishermen, a people used to the Duke's honor code. Leto's refusal to abandon Caladan's values on Arrakis is part of why the Imperial–Harkonnen trap works: he governs as if loyalty still binds a desert prize.

**Film (Villeneuve):** *Dune: Part One* opens on grey-green seas and the castle before the Heighliner lift. **Film (Lynch):** Caladan appears briefly as a wet contrast, then the story moves to Arrakis. Neither film is a substitute for the novel's household texture.
