The **Bene Gesserit** are a Sisterhood, not "witches" — that word is the popular slur. They train **prana-bindu** control, the **Voice**, and Truthsayers; they plant the **Missionaria Protectiva** in cultures they may later need. Reverend Mothers survive the spice agony and carry **Other Memory**. Their long game is a breeding program aimed at the **Kwisatz Haderach**.

Jessica's disobedience — a son for Duke Leto instead of the ordered daughter — arrives a generation early and unravels the plan. Reverend Mother Gaius Helen Mohiam tests Paul (gom jabbar) and speaks for the Order at court.

**Book:** interior politics, the agony, and genetic metaphysics are densest in Herbert. **Film (Villeneuve):** the Sisterhood is foregrounded as a political actor; Jessica and Mohiam remain central. The television series *Dune: Prophecy* is **Series**, not Book — one optional labeled track only. **Film (Lynch):** Voice and the gom jabbar remain; do not treat Lynch inventions as Sisterhood canon.

Shared: the Order, the Voice, Jessica's broken instruction. How far the breeding metaphysics go is Book-primary.
