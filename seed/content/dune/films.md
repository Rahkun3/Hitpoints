## Overview

Two major theatrical lines dominate discussion: David Lynch's 1984 feature and Denis Villeneuve's multi-part 2020s cycle. Each has its own pacing, design language, and plot emphasis. Label Film when they diverge from Book-primary lore.

## Lynch (1984)

Single-film adaptation of *Dune* with a distinctive visual and narrative approach. Treat as its own Film continuity when comparing to Herbert or to later adaptations.

## Villeneuve (2021 / 2024)

*Dune: Part One* (2021) and *Dune: Part Two* (2024) cover the first novel across two theatrical releases, with deliberate pacing and large-scale desert imagery.

## Continuity notes

> **Film continuity:** Adaptations, not literary replacements. Prefer Book for deep lore; cite Film when visuals, casting, or pacing differ.
