The **Fremen** are the desert people of Arrakis: sietch communities, stillsuit water-discipline, and a culture built around scarcity, sand, and **Shai-Hulud**. Sayyadina and Reverend Mothers hold ritual authority. Worm-riding and the prophecy of **Lisan al-Gaib** / Mahdi — planted by the Bene Gesserit **Missionaria Protectiva** — shape how tribes meet offworlders. Shared core: desert natives, water law, worm reverence, and the name **Muad'Dib** attaching to Paul.

**Book:** Liet-Kynes's ecological dream of a green Arrakis; Fedaykin as Paul's death-commandos; a jihad Paul foresees and cannot stop. Chani is Liet's daughter. How faith, ecology, and war lock together is Book-primary.

**Film (Villeneuve):** a North (skeptical, Chani) versus South (fundamentalist) political geography — Film-only; do not present it as Book. **Film (Lynch):** Fremen culture is abbreviated; stillsuits and prophecy remain as shorthand.

Do not collapse Lisan al-Gaib into Kwisatz Haderach: one is a planted Fremen prophecy, the other a Sisterhood breeding goal. They meet in Paul; they are not the same instrument.
