## Overview

Stilgar embodies Fremen law, water discipline, and sietch politics. He accepts Paul into tribal life and later follows him as faith and desert war escalate. Themes of loyalty and belief run Book-primary across Herbert's arc; films give him strong shared screen presence without replacing the novels' interior weight.

## Continuity notes

> **Book-primary:** Prefer Herbert for Stilgar's faith arc and long loyalty. Films share the Naib who accepts Paul — mark Film where pacing or emphasis diverge.
