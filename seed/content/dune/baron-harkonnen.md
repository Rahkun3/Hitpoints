Baron Vladimir Harkonnen is **Siridar Baron** of Giedi Prime and, with the Padishah Emperor, architect of the Arrakis trap that destroys House Atreides. He is uncle to Glossu Rabban and Feyd-Rautha. Through a Bene Gesserit secret he is also Jessica's father — hence grandfather to Paul and Alia. Villeneuve's *Dune: Part Two* states this Harkonnen parentage on screen as well.

**Book:** obese, suspensor-borne, sexually predatory; the court is a slave-culture of cruelty and appetite. The war is spice profit and Atreides annihilation, not a personal duel with Leto. After the ducal fall he reinstalls Harkonnen control through Rabban, holding Feyd-Rautha as a sharper successor.

**Film (Lynch):** boils and heart plugs — Lynch-only design, not Book. **Film (Villeneuve):** massive, floating, less camp, more brutal; much of the sexual-predation texture is omitted. Do not describe Lynch boils as Book, and do not import heart plugs into Herbert or into Villeneuve.

Shared core: Harkonnen baron, Giedi Prime, the trap. How the body and the appetite are shown is continuity-split.
