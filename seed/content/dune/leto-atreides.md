Duke Leto Atreides I is ducal head of **House Atreides**, father of **Paul**, and lord of **Caladan** before the Emperor assigns him Arrakis. His Mentat-adjacent court includes Thufir Hawat (Mentat-Master of Assassins), Duncan Idaho (Swordmaster), Gurney Halleck (Warmaster), and Suk doctor Wellington Yueh. Lady Jessica is his **bound concubine, not his wife**. He refuses marriage so a political match remains open and so her son's claim stays weaker than a legitimate heir's — a tool he accepts while he loves her.

The Arrakis transfer is a trap run with Baron Vladimir Harkonnen. Yueh betrays the House. Captured, Leto bites a poison-gas tooth Yueh planted for the Baron.

**Book:** the gas is residual; the Baron survives the attempt and Leto dies. **Film (Villeneuve):** the tooth is staged in the same trap logic — a last strike from a dying duke. Do not add Lynch's heart-plug to this death. **Film (Lynch):** separate devices and staging; not Book.

Leto's fall sends Jessica and Paul into the desert and ends Atreides rule in Arrakeen.
