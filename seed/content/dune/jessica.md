## Overview

Jessica's conflict is obedience versus attachment: the Sisterhood's plan versus the child she chose to bear for love. Voice, prana-bindu control, and political instinct make her indispensable to Atreides survival after the fall on Arrakis. Shared across Book and Film; interior motives and later-arc roles are Book-primary.

## Continuity notes

> **Book-primary:** Prefer Herbert for Jessica's Order conflict and long arc. Villeneuve films retain the core love-vs-obedience beat with their own visual emphasis.
