## English / publisher order

Standard English / publisher reading order (not Polish publication order, not strict in-universe chronology):

1. *The Last Wish*
2. *Sword of Destiny*
3. *Blood of Elves*
4. *Time of Contempt*
5. *Baptism of Fire*
6. *The Tower of the Swallow*
7. *The Lady of the Lake*
8. *Season of Storms* (main plot sits among the early stories; **epilogue is after the saga**)
9. *Crossroads of Ravens* (young-Geralt prequel; written for readers who already know him)

Do not skip the collections: Ciri's origin is in the short stories. Games and Netflix are separate continuities.

## Continuity notes

> **Book guide:** Publisher / English order; not Polish publication or strict chronology. Prefer this path before diving into CDPR or Netflix.
