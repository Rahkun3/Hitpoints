## Overview

Original language is **Polish**. English translations: Danusia Stok (*The Last Wish*, *Blood of Elves*); David French (most other volumes). Sapkowski: only his printed books are canon; adaptations are separate.

## Nine core volumes

English / publisher reading order:

1. *The Last Wish* — stories (official book 1)
2. *Sword of Destiny* — stories (introduces Ciri)
3. *Blood of Elves* — saga 1
4. *Time of Contempt* — saga 2
5. *Baptism of Fire* — saga 3
6. *The Tower of the Swallow* — saga 4
7. *The Lady of the Lake* — saga 5
8. *Season of Storms* — standalone midquel; epilogue after the saga
9. *Crossroads of Ravens* — young-Geralt prequel (PL 2024; EN 2025)

Polish publication order differs (*Sword of Destiny* before *The Last Wish*). Full path: [[reading-order|Reading order]].

## Continuity notes

> **Book continuity:** Sapkowski literary canon; nine core volumes in the English cycle. Games and Netflix are adaptations, not merges.
