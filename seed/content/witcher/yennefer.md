## Overview

Transformed by magic into the violet-eyed figure of legend, Yennefer navigates politics, desire, and power with little patience for soft myths about destiny — even as destiny keeps tying her to the White Wolf and the Lion Cub.

## Continuity notes

> **Shared core:** Djinn binding, sorceress identity, Ciri bond. Romance and political arcs differ across books, games (player choice), and Netflix.
