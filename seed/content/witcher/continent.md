## Overview

Fans call the setting **the Continent**; Sapkowski never published an official map (he has called some fan maps "mostly accurate"). Rough layout:

- **North:** Northern Kingdoms (Nordlings) — Redania, Temeria, Kaedwen, Aedirn, Cintra, Lyria & Rivia, and others — north of the Amell range and Yaruga / Pontar systems; west to the Great Sea.
- **South:** Nilfgaardian Empire, expanding northward.
- **West:** Skellige archipelago.
- **Toussaint:** autonomous Nilfgaardian vassal duchy (wine, knights-errant).
- **East / far south:** Korath, Zerrikania, Ofir, and related lands — traveler lore, thin cartography.

Politics: fractured North versus imperial South, with nonhumans (elves, dwarves, dryads) squeezed between. Major sites include Novigrad, Vizima, Oxenfurt, Cintra, the City of Golden Towers, and [[kaer-morhen|Kaer Morhen]]. CDPR maps emphasize playable slices, not a full surveyed world.

## Continuity notes

> **Shared setting:** No official Sapkowski map; fan and game maps are approximations. Names and regions recur across Book, Game, and Netflix — plot and borders can differ.
