## Overview

He works as a hired monster hunter and tries to stay out of politics; the nickname Butcher of Blaviken comes from a lesser-evil massacre the town misunderstood. He claims not to believe in destiny, then keeps invoking the Law of Surprise — which delivers Ciri. Bound by a djinn wish to Yennefer.

## Continuity notes

Book continuity ends with an ambiguous Avalon-like coda; CDPR's games continue him after the saga with amnesia. Netflix is a separate adaptation.

> **Book-primary:** Prefer Sapkowski's portrayal for identity and relationships. Game events after the saga are CDPR continuity. Netflix casting and plot are parallel, not merges.
