## Overview

Across Book, Game, and Netflix she is the hinge of the saga: hunted for her bloodline, capable of walking between worlds, and bound to Geralt and Yennefer as found family. Exact paths diverge by continuity — treat game endings and Netflix arcs as separate tracks.

## Continuity notes

> **Shared core:** Law of Surprise, Elder Blood, and the Geralt–Yennefer bond. Game choices (especially Wild Hunt endings) and Netflix's timeline are not interchangeable with the novels.
