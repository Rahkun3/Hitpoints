**Stormwind** is the human kingdom in the southern Eastern Kingdoms. In **RTS** *Warcraft: Orcs & Humans*, the southern human kingdom is called **Azeroth**, with capital Stormwind. Later sources use Azeroth for the planet and Stormwind for the kingdom. State both; do not collapse them.

**RTS:** Stormwind falls in the First War; refugees flee to Lordaeron. **Anduin Lothar** (the Lion of Azeroth) leads the survivors north. They fight the Second War (WC2) under the Alliance of Lordaeron; Stormwind is rebuilt after that war. *Warcraft III* barely uses Stormwind; that campaign focuses on Lordaeron and Kalimdor.

**WoW:** Stormwind City is the Alliance capital. House **Wrynn** (Llane to Varian to Anduin) is the ruling line players meet. **Varian**'s death in the Legion era is **WoW**-only.
