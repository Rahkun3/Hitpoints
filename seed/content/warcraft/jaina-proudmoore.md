**Jaina Proudmoore** is the daughter of Admiral **Daelin Proudmoore** of Kul Tiras and an apprentice of **Antonidas** in Dalaran.

**RTS / WC3:** she accompanies **Arthas Menethil**, refuses the Culling of Stratholme, sails to Kalimdor, and allies with **Thrall** at Hyjal. **Theramore** is founded after Hyjal, as a post-war Alliance settlement — not before the battle. *The Frozen Throne:* Daelin attacks the new Horde; Jaina cannot stop the conflict; Daelin dies.

**WoW / Novel:** Theramore is destroyed by Garrosh's mana bomb (*Tides of War* / Mists of Pandaria era). Later she leads the Kirin Tor, becomes Lord Admiral of Kul Tiras, and figures in Battle for Azeroth -- all **WoW**, kept brief.

In WC3 she is a Kirin Tor-trained sorceress; Kirin Tor leadership as an office is later **WoW**. She is not a 2016 **Film** character. Do not treat Theramore's fall or later admiralty as unmarked WC3 campaign facts.
