**Orgrimmar** sits in **Durotar**, Kalimdor. **Thrall** founds the settlement after the Third War and names it for **Orgrim Doomhammer**.

**RTS / TFT** shows the Horde settling Durotar in the Rexxar campaign (Founding of Durotar). That is a founding story, not the later city as a raid hub. The new orc nation is meant to hold a place on Kalimdor after Hyjal, opposite the Alliance presence at Theramore. TFT does not present valley districts or a player capital.

**WoW:** Orgrimmar is the Horde capital players use, laid out in valley districts, opposite **Stormwind**. The Siege of Orgrimmar (Mists of Pandaria era) is **WoW**-only. Do not describe that siege, Garrosh as Warchief, or later Horde politics as WC3 campaign geography. See **Azeroth** for planet-scale geography and **Warcraft III** for the Third War that precedes the founding.
