**Arthas Menethil** is crown prince of Lordaeron and a paladin of the Silver Hand, trained by **Uther** and close to **Jaina Proudmoore** in *Warcraft III*.

**RTS:** sent with Jaina and Uther to investigate the plague, he orders the Culling of Stratholme, takes **Frostmourne** in Northrend, kills King **Terenas**, and becomes a death knight of the Scourge. He destroys Quel'Thalas, raises **Sylvanas**, summons **Kel'Thuzad**, and enables **Archimonde** at Dalaran. In *The Frozen Throne* he reaches Icecrown, defeats Illidan, and becomes the Lich King (merging with **Ner'zhul**).

**Novel:** *Arthas: Rise of the Lich King* (Christie Golden, 2009) expands that RTS arc. It is not a separate ending.

**WoW:** Wrath of the Lich King era. He is defeated at Icecrown Citadel; **Bolvar Fordragon** takes the helm. That death and succession are MMO-era facts, not *Warcraft III* campaign text.
