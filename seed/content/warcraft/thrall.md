**Thrall**, son of **Durotan**, is Warchief of the reformed Horde in *Warcraft III*. **RTS:** raised as a slave by Aedelas Blackmoore at **Durnholde**, he meets **Grommash Hellscream** and **Orgrim Doomhammer**, and leads a reformed Horde. He follows the Prophet (**Medivh**) to Kalimdor. Grom drinks Mannoroth's blood, then redeems that curse. Thrall fights at Hyjal and founds **Durotar** and **Orgrimmar**.

**Novel / TBC:** *Lord of the Clans* (Christie Golden) is the main origin text before Warcraft III. It adds **Taretha Foxton** and **Draka**. The birth name **Go'el** is Novel / WoW (*The Burning Crusade* bloodline, Nagrand), not the WC3 campaign name.

**WoW:** he later steps down as Warchief (Garrosh succeeds him) and acts as shaman / World Shaman. Later Horde politics are **WoW**-only -- do not write them as WC3.

**Film:** infant Go'el and the Durotan plot in the 2016 movie are Film-track, not game biography.
