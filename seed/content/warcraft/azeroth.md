**Azeroth** is the world of the Warcraft setting. In **RTS** *Warcraft: Orcs & Humans* (1994), Azeroth also names the southern human kingdom whose capital is **Stormwind**. Later sources use Azeroth for the planet and Stormwind for that kingdom. Keep both senses; do not collapse them.

**RTS** geography of the Eastern Kingdoms includes **Lordaeron**, **Khaz Modan**, **Quel'Thalas**, and Stormwind/Azeroth. **Kalimdor** is the destination of the Third War in *Warcraft III*. **Northrend** is visited in Reign of Chaos (Frostmourne) and again in *The Frozen Throne*. Those three landmasses are the RTS map of Azeroth. **Outland** is a separate world (the remains of Draenor), not a continent of Azeroth. It first appears in *Warcraft III: The Frozen Throne* (Illidan / Draenei / Outland campaign). *Warcraft II: Beyond the Dark Portal* is Draenor before it is shattered.

**WoW** adds further continents and islands as expansion eras -- Pandaria, the Broken Isles, the Dragon Isles, Khaz Algar, and others. Name them as WoW-era geography; they are not the WC3 campaign map. *The Burning Crusade* is the MMO return to Outland, not its first appearance. Titan ordering and worldsoul cosmology belong to **WoW** / Chronicle (2016+) if mentioned at all, not to WC3 campaign text.
