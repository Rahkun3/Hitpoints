In Tolkien's books, Galadriel is the daughter of Finarfin and Eärwen, born in Valinor, who came to Middle-earth in the First Age. Accounts of her exact role in the rebellion of the Noldor vary across Unfinished Tales and later notes. She married Celeborn. Some texts place her in Eregion for a time. She bears Nenya, one of the Three made by Celebrimbor; she did not forge the One. In The Lord of the Rings she is Lady of Lothlórien: she shelters the Fellowship, tests them, and refuses the One Ring when Frodo offers it. After the War she sails West.

In Jackson's films, Cate Blanchett plays her as prologue narrator. The Ring-offer is staged as a dark visual temptation. The Hobbit films add White Council and Dol Guldur action beyond the novel.

In Amazon's series, Morfydd Clark plays a younger Second Age Galadriel as a driven commander seeking Sauron — a Series characterization. In the books she is not that warrior-revenge protagonist. Licensed games that place her in Mordor plots are Game inventions.
